Why this masterclass

Your hands decide the outcome.

Knowing the diagnosis is only half the job. What you do with your hands in the next twenty minutes is what the patient feels. This masterclass is about making that part deliberate, repeatable and safe.

  • ✓See it, don’t just hear it. Techniques are demonstrated live and explained step by step.
  • ✓Reasoning before force. Learn why a technique fits a presentation, and when to leave it alone.
  • ✓Ask in real time. Bring your own patient questions to the live discussion.
What we’ll cover

Skills you can use in clinic.

Four practical ideas, taught to be applied, not just remembered.

01

Read the movement first

See how the joint, the muscle chain and the patient’s own pattern explain the pain, before a hand touches the skin.

02

Place your hands with intent

Hand position, pressure and direction, demonstrated live so you can repeat them with confidence.

03

Choose the technique, then the dose

Know when to mobilise, when to release and when to leave a tissue alone, with the reasoning behind each choice.

04

Turn one session into a plan

Connect what you find today to the weeks of treatment ahead, and take your questions to the live discussion.
